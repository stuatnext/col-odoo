# COL Sales & Delivery

This add-on turns native Odoo CRM and Project into the day-to-day commercial
and delivery workspace for COL / FlareFlow.

## What the team sees

- **Commercial > Sales Pipeline**: native Odoo CRM pipeline, with COL fields
  for deal type, title list, territory, platform rights, commercial model and
  commercial notes.
- **Commercial > Delivery Projects**: partner-facing launch projects linked to
  the sales opportunity and signed rights agreement.
- **Delivery checklist**: creating a delivery project adds a practical launch
  checklist covering commercial scope, rights, assets, localisation,
  integration, acceptance testing, launch and reporting.

## Installation

1. Install Odoo's standard **CRM** and **Project** applications.
2. Install **COL Content Rights & Avails**.
3. Install **COL Content Rights - CRM Bridge**.
4. Install **COL Sales & Delivery**.

## Operating workflow

1. Add a commercial opportunity and fill in the **Content Deal** tab.
2. Qualify titles through the rights / avails workflow before sending a
   proposal.
3. Create the agreement when the commercial terms are agreed.
4. From the opportunity or agreement, select **Create Delivery Project**.
5. Assign dates and owners to the delivery checklist, and set delivery health
   to **Needs attention** or **Blocked** when a decision is required.

Use Odoo's standard quotation and invoicing applications for formal offers,
invoices and collection. This module is the COL-specific layer that makes the
commercial record and delivery plan traceable back to titles and rights.
