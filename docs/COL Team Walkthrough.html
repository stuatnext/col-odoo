01 / COMMERCIAL

Start with the opportunity.

Every buyer, distributor, telco or platform is a CRM opportunity. The commercial owner records the expected value, next activity, expected close date and stage.

What makes COL different

The Content Deal tab also records title scope, territory, platform rights and commercial model. The team sees the actual proposed deal, not just a contact and an email trail.

Create the opportunity

Name the partner, owner and commercial objective.

Define the content deal

Add titles, territories, rights requested and commercial model.

Move the deal forward

Keep the next action and expected close date current. Attach proposals and term sheets to the record.

02 / RIGHTS

Check availability before offering content.

The Avails Finder checks a title against the requested territory, platform type and window. Rights grants record the commercial truth at title level once terms are agreed.

Ask the availability question

Choose territory, platform, language and date. The system returns suitable titles.

Review any conflict

An exclusivity warning is an escalation, not an automatic stop. The rights owner resolves the commercial decision.

Record the agreement

Attach the signed agreement and create the rights grants that define its scope.

03 / DELIVERY

Turn a signed deal into shared launch work.

Create the delivery project from the opportunity or agreement. The platform starts the team with the same structured checklist every time, then the project manager assigns people and dates.

Confirm scope and rights

Lock the title list, contract position and grant set-up.

Prepare content and integration

Receive assets, complete metadata and localisation, and take the partner through integration and testing.

Launch and establish reporting

Approve launch, then assign the reporting cadence and commercial review.

04 / OPERATING RHYTHM

Use one weekly cadence across the team.

The workflow only works when records stay current. Each function updates the part it owns; the platform makes those updates visible in the next review.

Commercial

Owns opportunity value, stage and customer next step.

Rights / Legal

Owns agreements, grants and conflicts.

Project Manager

Owns delivery health, milestone and dependencies.

Workstream Owner

Owns assigned tasks, dates and blockers.

Finance

Owns quotation, invoice and collection status.

First thirty days

Adopt the habit before adding complexity.

Start with a focused group of live opportunities and partner launches. Do not attempt to recreate every historic spreadsheet at once.

  • Set the CRM stages and named commercial owners.
  • Import a reviewed title catalogue.
  • Load active agreements and their current rights grants.
  • Create delivery projects for live partner launches.
  • Run a short weekly commercial, rights and delivery review.
