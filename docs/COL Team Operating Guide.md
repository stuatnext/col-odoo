# COL Team Operating Guide

## One working record per deal

Every prospective buyer, telco, distributor or platform starts as an
opportunity in **Commercial > Sales Pipeline**. The commercial owner keeps the
value, next activity, expected close date and stage current. The **Content
Deal** tab records the title list, territories, platform rights, commercial
model and the decision needed next.

Do not use a separate spreadsheet as the live deal tracker after the platform
is adopted. A spreadsheet can still be attached as supporting evidence, but
the Odoo opportunity is the source of truth.

## Check rights before offering content

Before an offer, use **Rights > Avails Finder**. It checks titles against the
territory, platform and date requested. Record the agreed commercial terms in
an agreement, then add the individual title-level rights grants. Treat a
conflict flag as an escalation to the rights owner; it is intentionally not an
automatic block because the team may need to renegotiate an existing deal.

## Hand off the deal to delivery

When terms are sufficiently agreed, create the delivery project from the
opportunity or signed agreement. The project is the shared tracker for the
whole team. It opens with a checklist covering:

1. Scope and title list
2. Contract and rights set-up
3. Content assets
4. Metadata and localisation
5. Partner integration
6. Acceptance testing
7. Launch approval
8. Reporting and commercial review

The project manager assigns an owner and due date to every task immediately.
Use **Blocked** on a task only when work cannot continue without a decision,
asset, contract answer or partner action. Add the exact decision needed in the
blocker note.

## Weekly cadence

- **Commercial review:** every open opportunity has a next activity and a
  realistic expected close date.
- **Rights review:** conflicts and agreements that need follow-up are resolved
  or assigned.
- **Delivery review:** each active project has a current health status, next
  milestone and no unowned overdue work.

## Ownership

| Role | Accountable record |
|---|---|
| Commercial owner | Opportunity, expected value, customer communication |
| Rights / legal owner | Agreement, rights grants, conflicts |
| Project manager | Delivery project, dates, health and dependencies |
| Workstream owner | Assigned task, status and blocker note |
| Finance owner | Odoo quotation, invoice and collection status |
